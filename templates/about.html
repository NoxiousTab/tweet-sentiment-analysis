About Project

Tweet Sentiment Analysis System classifies a tweet as Positive, Negative or Neutral.

Subject: 410245A – Information Retrieval
Modern Education Society's Wadia College of Engineering, Pune

How it works

  1. The tweet is cleaned: lower-case, URLs, @mentions, #, digits and punctuation removed.
  2. It is tokenised with NLTK and stop-words are dropped.
  3. TF-IDF turns the remaining words into a numeric vector.
  4. A Logistic Regression model predicts the sentiment.
